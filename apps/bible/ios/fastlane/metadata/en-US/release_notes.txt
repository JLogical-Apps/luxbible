• Reading a Bible plan day now works like the main reader. Your main toolbar sits at the bottom with your pinned shortcuts and menu, and a button before the passage moves to the next one or finishes the day. Tap the toolbar to switch Bibles.
• Study panels now open while reading a plan, using the same panels as the main Bible, and plan audio plays in its own panel alongside them.
• Share or download an active plan with your progress and target end date. When you import a file with progress, choose whether to continue from it or start fresh.
• Catching up, or already partway through? Complete Through Day N in a plan's menu marks every day up to the one you choose complete.
• New Navigation settings, also in More: show books and chapters as a list or a grid, hide recent positions, and choose whether the keyboard opens right away.
• Navigation now groups books into the Old and New Testaments and lists up to 20 recent positions.
• New Get Started and Advanced Tutorial checklists walk you through the basics and more advanced features. Start either one from More > Help.
• What's New in More > About shows the highlights of the latest update.

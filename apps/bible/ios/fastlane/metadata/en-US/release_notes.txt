• Lux now remembers your copy settings. After copying verses, tap Edit to include or leave out the reference and translation, and later copies follow your choice. Online translations always include both.
• The WEB is now the World English Bible Classic, which renders God's name as Yahweh where the previous edition used "the LORD".
• When your selected Bible covers only one Testament, such as the LXX, copied verses and the passages shown with your notes now use the text you're reading in the other Testament.
• Fixed a few crashes, including one during audio playback.

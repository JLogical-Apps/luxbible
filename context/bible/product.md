# Product

## Overview

Lux Bible is a modern Bible reading and study app for iPhone, iPad, and Android. It combines serious study tools with an interface designed to remain approachable, fast, and visually calm.

Lux is intended for people who want more study depth than a mainstream reading app without the complexity of academic Bible software. Its experience supports three overlapping ways of using Scripture:

- Reading with minimal distraction
- Capturing highlights, annotations, and notes
- Studying translations, original languages, cross-references, commentaries, and lexical resources

The name Lux is inspired by:

> "Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path."

## Audience

Lux is primarily designed for digitally fluent Bible readers and churchgoers, with a particular appeal to younger adults because of its aesthetics, interaction model, and power-user features.

Age is not a product boundary. The more meaningful audience traits are:

- Comfortable learning efficient gestures and keyboard-driven navigation
- Interested in a modern alternative to traditional Bible software
- Wants tools that adapt to reading, note-taking, or deeper study
- Values offline access, privacy, and an uncluttered experience

The three core user profiles are Reader, Note-taker, and Studier. These profiles also appear as toolbar presets in the app.

## Positioning

Lux aims to be:

- More study-capable and customizable than YouVersion
- More intuitive and less overwhelming than Logos
- More modern and easier to navigate than Blue Letter Bible
- Powerful enough for serious study and clean enough for everyday reading

Lux is broadly theologically neutral at the product level. Lux does not affirm or promote a particular doctrine, denomination, interpretive framework, or statement of faith. Included historical resources can reflect their authors' own traditions and viewpoints.

## Product Principles

### Scripture First

The Bible text remains the center of the experience. Toolbars and study surfaces stay out of the way until needed, and previews avoid unnecessarily interrupting the user's current passage.

### Depth Without Clutter

Original-language data, commentary, cross-references, comparison, search, and reference works should be available without making ordinary reading feel like academic software.

### Adaptable to the Reader

Contextual toolbars, presets, display controls, highlight styles, notebooks, and study panels let users shape Lux around how they read and study.

### Offline First

Core study datasets and bundled Bibles work without a connection. Features that depend on licensed online translations or streamed audio are clearly treated as online features.

### Private by Design

Lux requires no account and stores user-created content locally. It has no analytics, behavioral tracking, or crash reporting.

### Free

Lux is free and committed to staying that way:

- No ads
- No subscriptions
- No in-app purchases

### Editorial Neutrality

Lux provides texts and study resources without asserting that users must adopt a particular theological conclusion. The app can include resources from identifiable historical traditions while remaining neutral in its own editorial voice.

## Design Identity

The visual identity centers on:

- An open-book logo with light radiating outward
- A modern, technology-forward aesthetic
- Clean typography
- Light and dark themes
- Immersive reading with minimal persistent chrome
- Bottom sheets for quick interactions and persistent panels for sustained study

The interface takes inspiration from modern systems such as Uber Base while maintaining its own identity.

## Distribution

Lux is publicly distributed on:

- Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad
- Google Play for Android

Mac and Apple Vision availability inherited from Apple's compatibility system is not treated as an intentionally supported platform.

## Community

- Discord is the primary discussion and feedback channel.
- Instagram is used for tips and product updates.

Lux does not currently have structured in-app feedback collection, a public roadmap, or a public changelog.

## Website

[`luxbible.app`](https://www.luxbible.app) is the marketing site for Lux. Its purpose is to:

- Explain the reading, writing, and study experience
- Demonstrate the design through screenshots and short videos
- Publish practical articles about Bible reading, study methods, and Lux workflows
- Link to the App Store and Google Play
- Invite users to the Discord community

A public roadmap and changelog are possible future additions, not current commitments.

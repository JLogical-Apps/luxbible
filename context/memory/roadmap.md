# Exploratory Roadmap

The items in this file are ideas for Lux Memory. They are not commitments, scheduled work, or complete specifications.

## Core Experience

- A short onboarding flow that establishes a normal daily practice duration
- A focused Today experience that plans work within the user's available time
- Dynamic replanning after each activity based on its result and actual duration
- A library of selected passages organized into active, maintaining, saved, and paused material
- A Discover experience for people who need help choosing what to memorize
- Calm progress feedback centered on durable recall rather than points or streaks

## Selecting Scripture

Potential ways to add material include:

- Selecting a reference or browsing Scripture
- Sending a selection from Lux Bible
- Importing verses associated with a Lux Bible notebook
- Choosing individual passages from source-attributed collections
- Memorizing a chapter, book, or custom passage range
- Receiving a collection shared by a friend, family member, or group
- Adding custom text where translation availability requires it

Selecting a passage should not necessarily activate it immediately. Users may build a larger list while the scheduler introduces new material only when their daily practice has room.

## Learning and Review

Possible activities include:

- Reading a passage in context
- Listening and reading aloud
- Learning meaningful phrases progressively
- Progressive word removal
- First-letter prompts
- Full typing or writing
- Oral recitation with self-checking
- Reference recall in both directions
- Practicing transitions between adjacent verses
- Whole-passage recitation and periodic accuracy checks
- Targeted practice for recurring trouble spots

Assisted and recognition-based activities can support learning, but long-term progress should depend primarily on successful recall with decreasing help across spaced sessions.

## Garden of Memory

The garden metaphor may appear through subtle passage icons, progress language, or an optional garden view:

- Planted for initial learning
- Sprouting after early unaided recall
- Taking Root during spaced strengthening
- Flourishing during long-term maintenance
- Needs Tending when review or correction is appropriate
- Dormant when intentionally paused

The exact vocabulary and visual prominence remain open. The experience should avoid dead plants, guilt, resource currencies, or growth based only on opening the app.

## Extended Passages

Chapters and books may need dedicated support for:

- Phrase and verse chunks
- Transitions between chunks
- Cumulative recitation
- Random starting points
- Periodic complete recitation
- Correction of small wording changes that appear over time

## Lux Integration

- Memorize selected Scripture from Lux Bible
- Add notebook passages to Lux Memory through a reviewed import flow
- Study a memory passage in Lux Bible
- Share a consistent family identity across websites, social media, and community spaces

## Accounts and Accountability

Possible later capabilities include:

- An optional Lux Account shared across Lux products
- Cross-device backup or synchronization
- Private accountability partners or circles
- Shared Bible reading plans
- Shared Scripture memory goals
- User-controlled completion visibility
- Predefined encouragement without unrestricted messaging

The account-free experience should remain complete. Child accounts, family administration, public groups, free-form communication, and moderation require additional legal and product work.

## Supporting Lux

Lux may explore optional tips that support development, licensing, and hosting without unlocking features. Any implementation must remain transparent that support paid to JLogical LLC is not a tax-deductible charitable contribution and must follow platform payment rules.

## Open Questions

- How prominent should the garden metaphor be?
- Which activities are necessary for the first useful release?
- What review model best serves exact Scripture recall over years?
- How should long passages and passage transitions be scheduled?
- Which translations can legally be included or transferred from Lux Bible?
- How should curated collections remain useful while preserving editorial transparency?
- When should optional accounts, synchronization, and accountability be introduced?
- How should Lux validate scheduler improvements without routine behavioral analytics?

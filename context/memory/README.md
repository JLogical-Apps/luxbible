# Lux Memory Context

Lux Memory is an exploratory Scripture memorization app in the Lux product family. An initial app shell exists, while the memorization experience remains exploratory.

## Status Conventions

- `product.md` records the current product hypothesis. It can change as the concept is tested.
- `features.md` contains only implemented user-facing behavior in the current app shell.
- `technical.md` records early direction and known constraints, not final architecture.
- Everything in `roadmap.md` is exploratory. It is not a commitment, schedule, or complete specification.
- `research.md` separates controlled evidence, field practice, product signals, and changing policy references.
- `validation.md` turns important evidence gaps into privacy-respecting research questions.

## Maintenance

- Keep positioning and principles in `product.md`.
- Keep implemented user-visible behavior in `features.md` only after it exists.
- Keep architecture, data boundaries, and integration constraints in `technical.md`.
- Keep unimplemented experience ideas and open questions in `roadmap.md`.
- Keep sources and their product implications in `research.md`.
- Keep unproven assumptions and proposed studies in `validation.md`.
- Link to Lux Bible context instead of duplicating its feature or technical documentation.
